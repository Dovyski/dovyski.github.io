<section class="mission-section" id="approach">
  <div class="mission-section-heading"><span class="eyebrow">Operating principles / 02</span><h2>How I work</h2></div>
  <div class="principles-grid">
    <article><h3>Architecture serves change</h3><p>I work toward boundaries a team can understand and evolve as the product changes. Infrastructure choices matter when they improve delivery, reliability or user experience.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Improve the engineering system</h3><p>Tooling, review, documentation and training shape what a team can ship. I treat that workflow as a system worth designing and measuring.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Build team capability</h3><p>I share context and develop practices that let more people make sound technical decisions, rather than routing every hard problem through one person.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Stay close to the problem</h3><p>Research, healthcare software, enterprise products and games taught me to understand constraints before choosing a technology.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
